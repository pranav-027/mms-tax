# **UNIT 1: INCOME FROM HOUSE PROPERTY**

---

### **1. INTRODUCTION**

Income from House Property is the **second head of income** under the Income Tax Act, 1961, governed by **Sections 22 to 27**. It refers to the income earned by an assessee from a building or land appurtenant thereto, of which the assessee is the owner.

#### **Conditions for Chargeability under "Income from House Property":**

1. The property must consist of a **building or land appurtenant** thereto.
2. The assessee must be the **owner** of the property.
3. The property must **NOT** be used by the owner for the purpose of his own business or profession.

---

### **2. TYPES OF HOUSE PROPERTY**

#### **(A) Self-Occupied Property (SOP):**
- A property used by the owner for his own residence.
- **Annual Value = NIL**.
- Maximum **two properties** can be treated as self-occupied (from AY 2020-21).

#### **(B) Let-Out Property (LOP):**
- A property given on rent for residential or commercial use.
- Income computed based on actual rent received.

#### **(C) Vacant Let-Out Property (VLOP):**
- A let-out property that remains vacant for part or whole year.
- Treated as a rented house for tax purposes.

#### **(D) Deemed Let-Out Property (DLOP):**
- If an assessee owns more than 2 properties, additional ones are **deemed to be let out**.
- Taxed based on **notional rent** even if vacant.

---

### **3. KEY TERMS USED IN HOUSE PROPERTY COMPUTATION**

#### **(A) Municipal Valuation (MV):**
The value determined by the local municipal authority for the purpose of charging municipal tax.

#### **(B) Fair Rent (FR):**
The rent which a similar property fetches in the same locality.

#### **(C) Standard Rent (SR):**
The maximum rent that can be charged under the Rent Control Act.

#### **(D) Reasonable Letting Value (RLV) / Expected Rent:**
- Higher of **Municipal Valuation** or **Fair Rent**
- Subject to **Standard Rent** as the upper cap

```
Step 1: A = Higher of MV or FR
Step 2: B = Standard Rent
Step 3: RLV = Lower of A and B
```

#### **(E) Actual Rent (AR):**
The actual rent received or receivable from the tenant.

#### **(F) Gross Annual Value (GAV):**
- **Higher of RLV or Actual Rent**
- **Exception:** If actual rent is lower than RLV due to vacancy, GAV = Actual Rent.

#### **(G) Net Annual Value (NAV):**
```
NAV = GAV – Municipal Taxes paid by Owner
```

---

### **4. DEDUCTIONS UNDER SECTION 24**

Only **two deductions** are allowed:

#### **(a) Standard Deduction [Sec 24(a)]:**
- **30% of Net Annual Value (NAV)**
- Covers all expenses (repairs, insurance, collection charges, etc.)
- No other expenses can be claimed.

#### **(b) Interest on Borrowed Capital [Sec 24(b)]:**

| **Type of Property** | **Maximum Deduction** |
|---|---|
| Self-Occupied Property | ₹2,00,000 |
| Let-Out Property | No upper limit |

#### **Pre-Construction Period Interest:**
- Period from **date of borrowing** to the **end of preceding financial year** of completion.
- Total pre-construction interest is allowed in **5 equal annual installments** starting from the year of completion.

```
Pre-Construction Interest = Loan Amount × Rate × Period (in years)
Annual Deduction = (1/5) × Pre-Construction Interest
```

---

### **6. SPECIAL NOTES**

1. **Charges for Lift, Generator, Security, etc.** – Not taxed under House Property; instead under **Other Sources**.
2. **Municipal Taxes** – Allowed only when **paid by the owner** (not by tenant) and on **payment basis**.
3. **Vacancy Allowance [Sec 23(1)(c)]** – If property remains vacant for part of the year, only actual rent received is taxed.
4. **Two SOP Limit** – Maximum 2 properties can be claimed as Self-Occupied; balance treated as Deemed Let Out.

# **UNIT 2: INCOME FROM BUSINESS OR PROFESSION**

---

### **1. INTRODUCTION**

Income from **Profits and Gains of Business or Profession (PGBP)** is the **third head of income** under Sections **28 to 44**. It includes profits earned from any trade, commerce, manufacture, or any concern in the nature of trade.

#### **Definitions:**

- **Business [Sec 2(13)]:** Any trade, commerce, manufacture, or adventure in nature of trade.
- **Profession [Sec 2(36)]:** An occupation requiring intellectual or specialized skill (e.g., CA, doctor, lawyer).

---

### **2. INCOMES CHARGEABLE UNDER PGBP [SECTION 28]**

1. Profits and gains from any business or profession.
2. Compensation received on termination of agency.
3. Income from specific services rendered to its members by trade/professional associations.
4. Export incentives (Duty Drawback, Cash Compensatory Support, etc.).
5. Value of any benefit or perquisite received.
6. Profits on sale of import licenses (REP licenses).
7. Interest, salary, bonus, commission received by partner from firm.
8. Sum received under Keyman Insurance Policy.
9. Speculative business profits (treated separately).

---

### **3. EXPENSES ALLOWED AS DEDUCTION**

#### **General Conditions:**
1. Expense must be incurred **wholly and exclusively** for business.
2. Should be of **revenue nature** (not capital).
3. Should not be a **personal expense** of the assessee.
4. Should not be **illegal**.

#### **Common Allowed Expenses:**
- Rent, Rates, Taxes, Repairs of Premises [Sec 30]
- Repairs of Plant, Machinery & Furniture [Sec 31]
- Depreciation [Sec 32]
- Salary, Bonus, Commission to employees
- Bad Debts actually written off [Sec 36(1)(vii)]
- Interest on borrowed capital
- Bonus & Commission to employees [Sec 36(1)(ii)] – on **payment basis** [Sec 43B]
- GST, Sales Tax (paid before filing return)
- Insurance Premium
- Advertising Expenses (legitimate business advertising)

---

### **4. EXPENSES NOT ALLOWED (DISALLOWED)**

1. **Personal Expenses** of proprietor.
2. **Income Tax & Wealth Tax** paid.
3. **Capital Expenditure** (treated as asset; only depreciation allowed).
4. **Donations** (claimed under Sec 80G separately).
5. **Provisions/Reserves** for bad debts, gratuity, etc. (only actual write-off allowed).
6. **Penalties / Fines** for violation of law.
7. **Bribes & Illegal payments** (specifically disallowed).
8. **Advertisement** in souvenir of political party.
9. **Interest on capital of proprietor** (drawn from own funds).

---

### **5. SECTION 43B – DEDUCTIONS ON ACTUAL PAYMENT BASIS**

The following are allowed as deductions **only when actually paid** before due date of filing return:
- GST, Customs Duty, Excise Duty
- Bonus or Commission to employees
- Employer's contribution to PF, Superannuation Fund, Gratuity Fund
- Interest on loans from banks/financial institutions
- Leave encashment to employees

---

### **6. SECTION 32 – DEPRECIATION**

#### **Conditions:**
1. Asset must be **owned** (wholly or partly) by assessee.
2. Asset used for the purpose of **business or profession**.
3. Depreciation is computed on **WDV (Written Down Value)** method.

#### **Block of Assets:**
- Assets are grouped into "blocks" based on similar nature and depreciation rate.
- **50% depreciation** if asset used **less than 180 days** in the year.

### **8. SPECIAL POINTS**

1. **Embezzlement by employee** = Allowed as business loss.
2. **Salary to spouse** = Allowed if she works in business actively.
3. **Bonus** = Allowed only when actually paid (Sec 43B).
4. **GST** = Allowed only when paid before due date (Sec 43B).
5. **Bribe** = Disallowed (illegal payment).
6. **Refund of Income Tax** = Not chargeable (already deducted earlier).

# **UNIT 3: CAPITAL GAINS**

---

### **1. INTRODUCTION**

**Capital Gains** is the **fourth head of income** under Sections **45 to 55A** of the Income Tax Act. It is the profit/gain arising from the **transfer of a capital asset**.

#### **Capital Asset [Sec 2(14)]:**
Property of any kind held by an assessee, whether or not connected with business — **excluding**:
1. Stock-in-trade
2. Personal effects (clothes, furniture)
3. Agricultural land in rural areas
4. Specified gold bonds

---

### **2. TYPES OF CAPITAL GAINS**

#### **(A) Short-Term Capital Gain (STCG):**
Asset held for **less than 24 months** (or 12 months for shares/MFs).

#### **(B) Long-Term Capital Gain (LTCG):**
Asset held for **more than 24 months** (or 12 months for shares/MFs).

---

### **3. HOLDING PERIOD FOR LONG-TERM CAPITAL ASSETS**

| **Asset Type** | **Holding Period** | **Tax Rate** |
|---|---|---|
| Listed Equity Shares | 12 months | 12.5% (above ₹1.25 lakh exemption) |
| Equity Mutual Funds | 12 months | 12.5% (above ₹1.25 lakh exemption) |
| Property (Land/Building) | 24 months | 12.5% (without indexation) |
| Gold / Gold ETF | 24 months | 12.5% |
| Debt Mutual Funds (post Apr 2023) | Any period | As per slab |

---

### **4. CALCULATION OF LTCG**

#### **Step-by-Step Process:**

**Step 1:** Determine **Full Value of Consideration** (Sale Price)

**Step 2:** Compute **Net Sale Consideration**
```
Net Sale Consideration = Sale Price – Transfer Expenses
```

**Step 3:** Compute **Cost of Acquisition** (Indexed if applicable)
```
Indexed Cost of Acquisition = Cost × (CII of Year of Sale / CII of Year of Purchase)
```

**Step 4:** Deduct exemptions under Sec 54/54B/54D/54EC/54F.

**Step 5:** Compute **LTCG chargeable to tax**
```
LTCG = Net Sale Consideration – Indexed Cost – Cost of Improvement – Exemptions
```

**Step 6:** Apply **Tax Rate**
- **12.5%** without indexation (default)
- **20%** with indexation (only for resident individuals/HUF on land/building purchased before 23.07.2024)

---

### **5. INDEXATION BENEFIT**

- Available **only for resident individuals & HUF** on assets purchased **before 23rd July 2024**.
- **Removed** for all other assets after Budget 2024.
- Adjusts purchase price for inflation using **Cost Inflation Index (CII)**.

---

### **6. EXEMPTIONS UNDER LONG-TERM CAPITAL GAINS**

| **Section** | **Asset Sold** | **Investment Required** | **Max Exemption** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sec 54 | Residential Property | Residential Property | ₹10 Crore |
| Sec 54B | Agricultural Land | New Agricultural Land | Capital Gain |
| Sec 54D | Industrial Land/Building | New Land/Building | Capital Gain |
| Sec 54EC | Land/Building | NHAI/REC Bonds | ₹50 Lakh |
| Sec 54F | Any other asset | Residential Property | Proportionate |
| Sec 112A | Equity Shares/Funds | NA | ₹1.25 Lakh |

---

### **7. CAPITAL GAINS ACCOUNT SCHEME (CGAS)**

- If unable to invest before due date of filing ITR, deposit funds in **CGAS account**.
- Allows time to invest later while still claiming exemption.
- Deposit must be made **before** the ITR filing due date.

# **UNIT 4: INCOME FROM OTHER SOURCES**

---

### **1. INTRODUCTION**

Income from **Other Sources** is the **fifth and residuary head** of income under **Section 56** of the Income Tax Act. It covers all income not classified under Salary, House Property, Business, or Capital Gains.

---

### **2. INCOMES CHARGEABLE UNDER OTHER SOURCES**

1. **Bank Interest** (Savings, FD, RD)
2. **Dividends** (from Indian/Foreign companies)
3. **Family Pension**
4. **Lottery & Game Show Winnings**
5. **Card Games, Betting, Gambling**
6. **Horse Race Winnings**
7. **Gifts** (above ₹50,000 from non-relatives)
8. **Royalty Income**
9. **Rent from Plant & Machinery** (not for business)
10. **Composite Rent** (Plant + Building – inseparable)
11. **Keyman Insurance Policy** (not in business)
12. **Interest on Income Tax Refund**
13. **Compensation on termination of employment**
14. **Director's Sitting Fees**

---

### **3. TAX RATES FOR INCOME FROM OTHER SOURCES**

| **Income Type** | **Tax Rate** |
|---|---|
| Casual Income (Lottery, Game Shows, etc.) | **Flat 30%** + cess |
| General Income (Interest, Dividend, etc.) | **Slab Rates** |

**Note:** Casual income has **no basic exemption** and **no deductions** allowed.

---

### **4. KEY EXEMPTIONS / DEDUCTIONS [SECTION 57]**

| **Income** | **Deduction** |
|---|---|
| Family Pension | Lower of **₹15,000** (₹25,000 New Regime) or **1/3rd of pension** |
| Dividend Income | Interest expense up to **20% of dividend** |
| Plant/Machinery Rent | Repairs, Insurance, Depreciation |
| Interest on Compensation | **50%** of such interest |

---

### **5. COMMON INCOMES – DETAILED TREATMENT**

#### **(A) Savings Bank Interest:**
- **Sec 80TTA:** ₹10,000 deduction for individuals & HUF (< 60 years).
- **Sec 80TTB:** ₹50,000 deduction for senior citizens.
- TDS not deducted by banks.

#### **(B) Fixed Deposit Interest:**
- Fully taxable at slab rates.
- TDS @ 10% if interest > ₹40,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens).

#### **(C) Family Pension:**
- Pension received by family of deceased employee.
- Standard deduction = Lower of ₹15,000 (₹25,000 New Regime) or 1/3rd of pension.

#### **(D) Lottery / Game Shows:**
- Flat **30% tax + 4% cess = 31.2%**
- No deductions allowed.
- TDS @ 30% if winnings > ₹10,000.

#### **(E) Dividend Income:**
- Taxable at slab rates.
- Interest expense deductible up to 20% of dividend.
- TDS @ 10% if dividend > ₹5,000 (₹10,000 from FY 2025-26).

#### **(F) Gifts:**
- **Cash > ₹50,000** from non-relatives → fully taxable.
- **Movable property without consideration > ₹50,000** → taxable.
- **Exempt:** Gifts from relatives, on marriage, by will/inheritance.

#### **(G) Income Tax Refund:**
- Refund itself → Not taxable.
- **Interest on refund** → Taxable under Other Sources.

---

### **6. EXPENSES NOT DEDUCTIBLE**

1. Personal expenses.
2. Capital expenditure.
3. Expenses for casual income (lottery, horse race, online gaming).
4. Interest on dividend > 20% of dividend.
5. Tax payments (Income Tax, Wealth Tax).
6. Expenses payable to non-residents without TDS.

---

### **7. DEDUCTIONS UNDER SECTION 80G – DONATIONS**

#### **Categories of Deduction:**

| **Category** | **Deduction** | **Examples** |
|---|---|---|
| 100% without limit | Full deduction | PM National Relief Fund, National Defence Fund |
| 50% without limit | Half deduction | Jawaharlal Nehru Memorial Fund, PM Drought Relief Fund |
| 100% with 10% AGTI limit | Subject to limit | Govt. for Family Planning |
| 50% with 10% AGTI limit | Subject to limit | Other charitable institutions |

#### **Conditions:**
1. Donation through **cheque, draft, or online** (Cash limit: ₹2,000).
2. **In-kind donations** (food, clothes) **NOT** eligible.
3. Donee institution must provide **Form 10BE**.
4. Available only under **Old Regime**.

---

### **8. RELATED SECTIONS**

| **Section** | **Purpose** | **Deduction** |
|---|---|---|
| **80GGA** | Scientific Research / Rural Development | 100% |
| **80GGB** | Companies' contribution to political parties | 100% |
| **80GGC** | Individuals' contribution to political parties | 100% |

### **NUMERICAL 1: Income from Self-Occupied / Let-Out Property**

**Q.** Mr. Parekh's bungalow has Municipal Valuation = ₹24,000; Rent realized = ₹21,000; Municipal Tax paid = ₹4,000; Cost of Repairs = ₹2,000; Collection Charges = ₹1,000; Insurance = ₹3,000. Compute Income from House Property.

**Solution:**

| **Particulars** | **Amount (₹)** |
|---|---|
| Gross Annual Value (Higher of RLV ₹24,000 or AR ₹21,000) | 24,000 |
| Less: Municipal Taxes paid by owner | (4,000) |
| **Net Annual Value (NAV)** | **20,000** |
| Less: Standard Deduction u/s 24(a) (30% of 20,000) | (6,000) |
| **Income from House Property** | **14,000** |

**Note:** Repairs, collection charges, insurance — all covered by Standard Deduction.

### **NUMERICAL 2: Let-Out Property with Pre-Construction Interest**

**Q.** Mr. Mehra owns let-out property in Delhi:
- Fair Rent = ₹24,000; Actual Rent = ₹30,000
- ₹10,000 received for lift, generator, security
- Municipal Tax paid = ₹4,000
- Loan: ₹40,000 borrowed on 1.4.2020 @ 10% used for construction; completed 31.3.2024
- Current year interest = ₹4,000

**Solution:**

| **Particulars** | **Amount (₹)** |
|---|---|
| Gross Annual Value (Higher of RLV ₹24,000 or AR ₹30,000) | 30,000 |
| Less: Municipal Taxes paid | (4,000) |
| **Net Annual Value** | **26,000** |
| Less: Standard Deduction (30% of 26,000) | (7,800) |
| Less: Interest on borrowed funds (current year) | (4,000) |
| Less: Pre-construction Interest (1/5) | (2,400) |
| **Income from House Property** | **11,800** |

**Working Notes:**
- **Pre-construction Interest** = ₹40,000 × 10% × 3 years = ₹12,000
- Annual deduction = 12,000 ÷ 5 = ₹2,400
- ₹10,000 (lift, generator, security) → taxed under **Other Sources** (not HP).

## **KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR EXAM PREPARATION**

1. **House Property:**
   - Master GAV → NAV → Income calculation format.
   - Standard Deduction = **30% of NAV** (only 2 deductions allowed under Sec 24).
   - Pre-construction interest = 1/5 in 5 years.
   - Max 2 SOPs allowed.

2. **Business / Profession:**
   - Always start with **Net Profit per P&L**.
   - **ADD** disallowed expenses and **DEDUCT** non-business incomes.
   - Remember **Sec 43B** (payment basis): GST, Bonus, PF, Interest, etc.
   - Disallow: Personal exp, Income Tax, Donations, Bribes, Capital expenditure, Provisions/Reserves.

3. **Capital Gains:**
   - LTCG holding period: **24 months** (property), **12 months** (shares).
   - **12.5% without indexation** (default after Budget 2024).
   - **20% with indexation** for residents on land/building purchased before 23.07.2024.
   - Sec 54 = ₹10 Cr cap; Sec 54EC = ₹50 lakh.
   - Sec 112A = ₹1.25 lakh exemption.

4. **Other Sources:**
   - Lottery taxed @ flat **30%** (no deductions).
   - Family pension: deduction = lower of ₹15,000/₹25,000 or 1/3.
   - Sec 80TTA = ₹10,000; Sec 80TTB = ₹50,000 (seniors).
   - Gift > ₹50,000 from non-relatives = taxable.
   - Dividend interest expense limited to 20%.

5. **Sec 80G – Donations:**
   - 100% / 50% with or without limits.
   - Cash limit: ₹2,000.
   - In-kind not allowed.
   - Available **Old Regime only**.

6. **Practice Numericals Repeatedly** – especially HRA, LTCG, House Property, and Business Income computations.

7. **Sectional Memorization** – all chargeability sections (22, 28, 45, 56) and key deduction sections (24, 30-32, 36, 37, 43B, 54, 80G, 80TTA, 80TTB).

8. **Format Practice** – maintain neat tabular formats in computational answers for higher marks.

9. **Exam Tip:** Always state assumptions clearly (regime chosen, ownership, residential status).

10. **Time Management:** Theory + Numerical balance is crucial — solve numericals first, then attempt theory and MCQs.
