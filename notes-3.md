# **UNIT 1: INTRODUCTION TO INCOME FROM SALARIES**

---

### **1. MEANING OF SALARY**

**Salary** is any remuneration paid by an **employer** to his **employee** on a regular basis in consideration for his services rendered. Income can be taxed under this head only when there exists a **relationship of employer and employee** for amounts received by the employee by virtue of employment, for services rendered under an **express or implied contract**.

#### **Essential Conditions for an Income to be Taxed as Salary:**

1. There must be an **employer-employee relationship** (master-servant relationship).
2. Payment should be made in consideration of services rendered.
3. The contract may be **express (written/oral)** or **implied**.
4. Payment may be made in **cash or kind** (monetary or non-monetary).

#### **Important Notes:**

- Any amount received from a person **other than the employer** cannot be termed as salary.
- **Example:** A Member of Parliament (MP) or MLA is **NOT** treated as a Government employee. Therefore, salary and allowances received by them are **NOT** chargeable to tax under "Income from Salary" — instead taxed under **"Income from Other Sources"**.
- The definition includes **monetary value** as well as **non-monetary value** of benefits and services provided by the employer.
- Salary forms the **most significant portion** of income for middle-income taxpayers in India.

---

### **2. SCOPE OF SALARY [Section 17(1)]**

The definition of salary under **Section 17(1)** is **inclusive in nature**, meaning even components not explicitly listed may be considered salary based on facts and circumstances.

#### **Components Included in Salary [Section 17(1)]:**

1. **Basic Pay** (Wages)
2. **Allowances:**
   - House Rent Allowance (HRA)
   - Leave Travel Allowance (LTA)
   - Children Education Allowance
   - Special Allowances
3. **Perquisites** – Benefits received in kind (accommodation, lunch, commute expenses, etc.)
4. **Variable Pay / Performance Bonus**
5. **Leave Encashment** – Consideration for unutilized leave days
6. **Pension or Annuity**
7. **Gratuity**
8. **Salary Advance**
9. **Compensation** received on termination or modification of employment contract
10. **Fees and Commission** (as part of employment contract)
11. **Taxable portion of employer's contribution to Provident Fund**

---

### **3. DIFFERENCE BETWEEN CTC AND TAKE-HOME SALARY**

#### **(A) Cost to Company (CTC):**
The **total cost** incurred by the company on an employee. It includes:
- Salary received each month
- Retirement benefits (PF, gratuity)
- Non-monetary benefits (cab service, medical insurance, free meals, phone bills reimbursed)

#### **(B) Take-Home Salary:**
The amount actually credited to the employee's bank account after deductions.

**Formula:**
```
Take-Home Salary = Gross Salary
                 – Provident Fund Deduction
                 – Medical Insurance Deduction
                 – Income Tax Payable (after Sec 80 deductions)
```

# **UNIT 2: COMPONENTS OF SALARY**

---

### **1. BASIC SALARY**

- **Fixed component** of the paycheck.
- Forms the **basis** for calculating other components.
- HRA, DA, and PF are calculated as a percentage of Basic Salary.
- Constitutes a **large portion** of total salary.
- **Fully Taxable**.

---

### **2. DEARNESS ALLOWANCE (DA)**

- Paid to compensate for inflation/cost of living.
- Two types:
  - **DA forming part of retirement benefits** – considered for HRA, PF, gratuity.
  - **DA not forming part of retirement benefits** – not included.
- **Fully Taxable**.

---

### **3. HOUSE RENT ALLOWANCE (HRA) – Section 10(13A)**

- Allowance given to meet **rental expenses**.
- Available **only under the Old Regime**.
- Cannot be claimed under New Tax Regime.

#### **Exemption (Least of the Following Three):**

1. **Actual HRA Received** from employer
2. **50% of (Basic + DA)** if living in **Metro Cities** (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata)
   **OR 40% of (Basic + DA)** if living in **non-metro cities**
3. **Actual Rent Paid – 10% of (Basic + DA)**

#### **Special Notes:**

- If you do **not live on rent**, HRA is **fully taxable**.
- HRA can be claimed even when **living with parents**, provided:
  - Rental agreement is signed
  - Rent paid via bank transfer
  - Rental receipts maintained
  - Parents declare rent as their **House Property income**.

---

### **4. LEAVE TRAVEL ALLOWANCE (LTA)**

- Exemption available for travel **within India** only.
- Exemption only for the **shortest distance** of the trip.
- Available for travel with **spouse, children, and parents** (NOT other relatives).
- Exemption is restricted to **actual expenses incurred**.
- Bills must be **submitted to employer**.
- Available for **2 journeys in a block of 4 calendar years**.

---

### **5. EMPLOYEE PROVIDENT FUND (EPF)**

- Social security initiative under **EPF Act, 1952**.
- Mandatory for companies with **20 or more employees**.
- Both employer and employee contribute **12% of basic salary** each.
- **Current EPF interest rate: 8.25%**.
- **TDS Rate** for taxable EPF withdrawals = **20% for non-PAN holders**.

---

### **6. BONUS**

- Paid once or twice a year.
- **100% Taxable**.
- Includes performance incentives, variable pay, etc.

---

### **7. PROFESSIONAL TAX**

- Levied by **State Governments** (not by Central Government).
- Maximum amount = **₹2,500 per annum**.
- Deducted by employer and deposited with state government.
- Allowed as **deduction from salary** under Old Regime only [Section 16(iii)].

### **1. DEDUCTIONS UNDER SECTION 16**

#### **(a) Standard Deduction [Section 16(ia)]**

| **Regime** | **FY 2024-25** | **FY 2025-26** |
|---|---|---|
| Old Regime | ₹50,000 | ₹50,000 |

- Available **only to Government Employees**.
- **Least of the following** is exempt:
  1. **1/5th of Basic Salary** (20%)
  2. **₹5,000** (Maximum)
  3. **Actual Allowance Received**

- Available under **Old Regime only**.
- Actual amount paid is allowed as deduction.

---

### **2. DEDUCTIONS UNDER CHAPTER VI-A**

#### **(a) Section 80C – Investment Deductions**

- Maximum deduction: **₹1,50,000** per annum.
- Available **only under Old Regime**.
- **Eligible Investments:** PPF, EPF, ELSS, LIC premium, NSC, Tax-saving FDs, Tuition fees, etc.

#### **(b) Section 80D – Medical Insurance**

| **For** | **Maximum Deduction** |
|---|---|
| Self & Family | ₹25,000 |
| Senior Citizens (Self/Family) | ₹50,000 |
| Parents (Non-senior) | ₹25,000 |
| Parents (Senior Citizens) | ₹50,000 |

- Available under **Old Regime only**.

#### **(c) Section 24 – Home Loan Interest**

- For **Let-out Property:** Interest on home loan **fully deductible** (irrespective of payment).
- For **Self-Occupied Property:** Maximum deduction = **₹2,00,000**.
- Self-occupied deduction available **only under Old Regime**.
- Let-out property deduction available under **both regimes**.

#### **(d) Section 80CCD – Pension Contribution (NPS)**

- **Section 80CCD(1):** Employee's own contribution → up to ₹1,50,000 (within 80C limit).
- **Section 80CCD(1B):** Additional ₹50,000 deduction.
- **Section 80CCD(2):** Employer's contribution up to **14% of basic salary** allowed under **both regimes**.

# **UNIT 4: EXEMPTIONS ON RETIREMENT BENEFITS**

---

### **1. GRATUITY [Section 10(10)]**

Gratuity is a retirement benefit provided by employer to employee. Eligibility = **5 years of continuous service**.

#### **(A) Government Employees:**

- **Fully Exempt** from tax.

#### **(B) Employees Covered Under Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972:**

Exemption = **LEAST of the following**:

1. **15 days' salary** for every completed year of service (or part > 6 months)
   = **(Salary Last Drawn × 15 × No. of Years) ÷ 26**
2. **₹20,00,000** (notified limit)
3. **Actual Gratuity Received**

**Note:**
- Salary = **Basic + DA**
- Working days per month = **26 days** (30 minus 4 Sundays)
- For **seasonal establishments**, **7 days** are taken instead of 15 days.
- Part of year > 6 months = considered as full year; ≤ 6 months = ignored.

#### **(C) Other Employees (Not Covered Under PGA, 1972):**

Exemption = **LEAST of the following**:

1. **Half month's average salary** for each completed year of service
   = **(Average Salary × 1/2 × No. of Completed Years)**
2. **₹20,00,000**
3. **Actual Gratuity Received**

**Note:**
- Average Salary = Average of last **10 months'** Basic + DA + Commission (fixed % on turnover).
- **Only completed years** counted (part year ignored, even if > 6 months).

---

### **2. PENSION [Section 10(10A)]**

Pension is a periodic payment to retired employee. It can be **uncommuted** (monthly) or **commuted** (lump sum).

#### **(A) Uncommuted Pension:**

- **Fully Taxable** for all employees (Govt. & Non-Govt.).

#### **(B) Commuted Pension:**

| **Type of Employee** | **Exemption** |
|---|---|
| Government Employee | **Fully Exempt** |
| Non-Govt. Employee (with Gratuity) | **1/3rd** of full pension exempt |
| Non-Govt. Employee (without Gratuity) | **1/2** of full pension exempt |

#### **Exemption Formula (Non-Govt.):**

```
Step 1: Calculate Full Pension = Commuted Amount × (100 ÷ % Commuted)
Step 2: Calculate Exempt Portion (1/3 or 1/2 of Full Pension)
Step 3: Taxable Pension = Commuted Amount – Exempt Portion
```

---

### **3. LEAVE ENCASHMENT [Section 10(10AA)]**

#### **(A) Government Employees:**

- **Fully Exempt** at the time of retirement.

#### **(B) Non-Government Employees:**

Exemption = **LEAST of the following**:

1. **Cash equivalent of leave** at the credit (max 30 days × completed years of service)
2. **10 months' average salary** (preceding 10 months)
3. **₹3,00,000** (Notified limit; some references mention ₹25 lakh – please verify per latest notification)
4. **Actual Leave Encashment Received**

**Note:** Earned leave should not exceed 30 days for every year of service.

---

### **4. VOLUNTARY RETIREMENT SCHEME (VRS) [Section 10(10C)]**

- Maximum exemption: **₹5,00,000**.

#### **Eligibility Conditions:**

1. Compensation received from:
   - Central / State Government
   - Local Authority
   - University, IIT, IIM
   - PSU, Company, Cooperative Society
2. Compliance with **Rule 2BA**.
3. Exemption can be claimed only in the **assessment year of receipt**.
4. If relief under **Section 89** is claimed, exemption under 10(10C) cannot be claimed.

---

### **5. RELIEF UNDER SECTION 89(1) – Arrears/Advance Salary**

Tax relief is available when:
- Salary is received in **arrears** or **advance**
- Family pension received in **arrears**

#### **Steps to Calculate Relief:**

| **Step** | **Action** |
|---|---|
| Step 1 | Tax on total income (incl. additional salary) for the year of receipt |
| Step 2 | Tax on total income (excl. additional salary) for the year of receipt |
| Step 3 | Difference (Step 1 – Step 2) |
| Step 4 | Tax on income (excl. arrears) for the year arrears relate to |
| Step 5 | Tax on income (incl. arrears) for the year arrears relate to |
| Step 6 | Difference (Step 5 – Step 4) |
| **Relief** | **Step 3 – Step 6** (if positive) |

**Note:** If Step 6 ≥ Step 3, **no relief** is allowed.

# **UNIT 7: TDS ON SALARY**

---

- TDS = **Tax Deducted at Source**.
- Employer deducts TDS monthly based on annual salary estimation.
- Form **16** (TDS certificate) issued by employer (June/July).

#### **Form 16 has Two Parts:**

- **Part A:** Employer & employee details, PAN, TAN, TDS deductions.
- **Part B:** Salary details, deductions allowed, tax payable.

#### **Form 26AS:**

- Summary of all **taxes deducted/paid** on behalf of taxpayer.
- Available on Income Tax Department portal.

#### **Annual Information Statement (AIS):**

Records all financial transactions:
- Salary, FD interest, capital gains, mutual fund transactions, sale/purchase of property, foreign remittances, cash deposits > ₹10 lakh, credit card payments > ₹10 lakh.

### **NUMERICAL 1: Basic Salary Computation**

**Q.** Miss Chitvan is employed with L&T Mindtree as software trainer. Compute her taxable salary for AY 2024-25.

- Basic Salary = ₹5,000 p.m.
- Taxable Conveyance Allowance = ₹3,500 p.m.
- DA: 25% of Basic from 1.4.2023 to 31.12.2023; 32% of Basic from 1.1.2024 to 31.3.2024.
- Professional Tax = ₹120 p.m.
- Bonus = 20% of (Basic + DA)
- Mediclaim Premium paid by employer = ₹2,800

**Solution:**

| **Particulars** | **Calculation** | **Amount (₹)** |
|---|---|---|
| Basic Salary | 5,000 × 12 | 60,000 |
| DA (Apr–Dec 2023) | 5,000 × 9 × 25% | 11,250 |
| DA (Jan–Mar 2024) | 5,000 × 3 × 32% | 4,800 |
| Conveyance Allowance | 3,500 × 12 (Note: example uses given figure) | 3,500 |
| Bonus | (60,000 + 16,050) × 20% | 15,210 |
| **Gross Taxable Salary** | | **94,760** |
| Less: Standard Deduction u/s 16 | | (50,000) |
| Less: Professional Tax | 120 × 12 | (1,440) |
| **Net Taxable Salary** | | **43,320** |

**Note:** Mediclaim premium paid by employer is an **exempt perquisite**.

- Gross Salary = ₹14,00,000
- Less: Std. Deduction = ₹75,000

**Tax Calculation:**
- Up to ₹3,00,000 = 0
- ₹3,00,001 – ₹7,00,000 (5% × 4,00,000) = ₹20,000
- ₹7,00,001 – ₹10,00,000 (10% × 3,00,000) = ₹30,000
- ₹10,00,001 – ₹12,00,000 (15% × 2,00,000) = ₹30,000
- ₹12,00,001 – ₹13,25,000 (20% × 1,25,000) = ₹25,000

**Total Tax = ₹1,05,000**
- Add: H&E Cess @ 4% = ₹4,200
- **Net Tax Payable = ₹1,09,200**

1. **Master Section 17(1)** – Definition of Salary and its inclusive components.

2. **Memorize Important Sections:**
   - Sec 10(5) – LTA
   - Sec 10(10) – Gratuity
   - Sec 10(10A) – Commuted Pension
   - Sec 10(10AA) – Leave Encashment
   - Sec 10(10C) – VRS
   - Sec 10(13A) – HRA
   - Sec 16 – Deductions from Salary
   - Sec 17 – Definition of Salary

3. **Practice HRA Calculation** – Always remember the **3-condition Least Test**.

4. **Gratuity Computation:**
   - PGA Employees → 15 days × salary × years ÷ 26
   - Non-PGA → 1/2 × avg salary × completed years
   - Govt. → Fully Exempt

5. **Pension Rules:**
   - Uncommuted = Fully Taxable
   - Commuted: Govt = Fully Exempt; Non-Govt = 1/3 (with gratuity), 1/2 (without)

6. **Standard Deduction:**
   - ₹50,000 (Old)
   - ₹75,000 (New)

7. **Rebate u/s 87A:**
   - ₹12,500 (Old, ≤ ₹5 lakh)
   - ₹60,000 (New, ≤ ₹12 lakh) for FY 2025-26

8. **Always remember Cess @ 4%** on (Tax + Surcharge).

9. **Practice Computation Format** systematically – Gross Salary → Deductions → Net Taxable Income.

10. **Compare Old vs New Regime** numerically for tax planning questions.

11. **Know Provident Fund Tax Treatment** – SPF, RPF, URPF, PPF differences.

12. **Form 16, Form 26AS, AIS** – Documents required for filing ITR.
